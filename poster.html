Project Abstract

Virtual House Designer is a browser-based application that makes house planning simple. Users enter their plot size, design a floor plan on an interactive grid, furnish every room, try furniture on a photo of a real room, and view the finished house in 3D, without architects or expensive software.

Objectives

  • Make house planning simple and affordable.
  • Design accurate floor plans on a grid.
  • Furnish rooms before buying anything.
  • Visualize the design in 3D.
  • Save, preview and export projects.
